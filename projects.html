Enough Food, Out of Reach: Zambia Food Security Tool

Built with Karabo for the Women in Data 2026 "What's Cooking" Datathon. The tool walks through four questions: is there enough food, can people afford it, could they grow it themselves, and can they reproduce the seed. I built the dataset behind it: 2,322 verified rows and 74 indicators across five FAOSTAT domains, spanning 1961 to 2025. I also wrote the schema Karabo developed against, so she could build the front end while the analysis was still running. Two import errors surfaced during validation and would have inverted the conclusions. I caught both. One check tested whether crop area times yield equalled production across 256 observations. The findings contradicted my starting hypothesis twice. Zambia's food supply exceeded national requirement in 2018, yet 83% of people could not afford a healthy diet. Availability was never the constraint. Cropland also runs a large nitrogen surplus at only 37% use efficiency, so more subsidised fertiliser alone would not solve the problem. The tool closes with a household calculator that turns national figures into one family's arithmetic.

MATH2016 Test 2 Prep — A Real Analysis Study Site

I built this site to prepare for Tutorial Test 2 in MATH2016, a real analysis course. The test has no appendix and asks for short answers, so I had to know every definition and proof from memory. Reading my notes did not get me there. I needed to understand why each result is true. The site explains Riemann integration, Darboux sums, Darboux's theorem and Taylor's theorem. It works through the class examples step by step. It also gives practice questions after each topic, a recall drill and a timed mock test. I kept it public so other students can use it. Based on the MATH2016 lecture notes. Built with help from Claude.
